# Nimbus Coffee Co. — Customer FAQ

> Nimbus Coffee Co. is a fictional specialty coffee roaster created for a tutorial. Every policy, address, and order in this file is made up.

## About Nimbus

Nimbus Coffee Co. is a small specialty roaster. We roast whole-bean coffee to order and ship it in 12 oz bags. We also offer an optional coffee subscription. We do not sell ground coffee or pods.

## Shipping

- We roast your coffee within 1 business day of your order, then ship it.
- US orders arrive in 3–5 business days after roasting.
- We ship to the US only. We do not ship internationally.
- Every order gets a tracking update when it leaves the roastery.
- Shipping is free on US orders of $40 or more. Orders under $40 pay a flat $6 shipping fee.

## Returns and refunds

- Unopened bags can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund or a replacement.
- Opened bags cannot be returned, unless the bag arrived damaged (see below).
- To start a return, email support@nimbus-coffee.example with your order number.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the bag.
- We cannot issue same-day refunds.

## Damaged, wrong, or missing bags

- If a bag arrives torn, crushed, or wrong, take a photo and email support@nimbus-coffee.example within 7 days of delivery.
- We send a free replacement. You do not need to return the damaged bag.
- If tracking says "delivered" but you have no package, wait 1 business day, then email us and we will open a carrier claim.

## Subscriptions

- A subscription ships one or two bags every 2 or 4 weeks.
- You can skip, pause, or cancel any time before your next roast date. Your roast date is shown in your Nimbus account.
- Changes made after the roast date apply to the following shipment.
- Our support assistant cannot change subscriptions for you. Please use your Nimbus account page.

## Tasting room

- Address: 12 Harbor Lane, Portland, Maine (fictional).
- Hours: Monday to Friday 8:00 am – 4:00 pm, Saturday 9:00 am – 2:00 pm. Closed Sunday.

## Decaf and allergens

- We sell one decaf: Night Owl Decaf, decaffeinated with the Swiss Water Process.
- Our coffee contains only roasted coffee beans. Our roastery does not handle nuts, dairy, or gluten.
- We cannot give medical advice. If you have a health concern, please ask your doctor.

## Wholesale

- Cafés and offices can apply for wholesale at wholesale@nimbus-coffee.example.
- Wholesale pricing is quoted individually. We do not publish wholesale discounts.

## Order status

- To check an order, have your order number ready. Order numbers look like NMB- followed by four digits, for example NMB-1042.
- Our support assistant can look up the status of an order by its number.
